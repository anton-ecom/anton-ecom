# N-ORG: Network Organization

## Intro:

There’s a lot going on in technology innovations. New things pop-up every day with major breakthroughs in AI, connectivity and automation that dramatically change our everyday lives. 

However, we keep working within 20th century organisational models that are designed for manufacturing and monopolies. Division of labour, vertical top-down management, silos and fiefdoms of departments - it works so far and until processes are fixed and pre-determined. Most importantly - they are created for a world with lower complexity, than it is today.  

In the recent decade, it is beginning to change. The pressure of limitations of current organisational technology is becoming evident, but sociatal patterns are harder to change. They always lag behind the technology and most of the time - and as we see in AI - decide how we use the technology. 

What's evident, that with the rise of communication and globalization, old structures are slowing down innovations and more often choose economies of scale, rather than scale of innovations. Large companies are becoming highly inefficient with organisation structures and management putting a heavy burden on companies' abilities to grow, innovate and provide meaning and purpose to their employees. Corporate structure becomes so rigid, that even large shake-ups and market changes cannot affect them. As a result, companies are making more of the same and are constantly fighting back the system, lobbying and resisting change - holding tight for the old - even if it no longer benefits people. Not a single large company has avoided that structural fundamentalism, including most advanced companies like Apple, Google and Microsoft. Organizational design is overlooked and developments in this direction is slow.   



---
## Case in point: AI Data-centers

We've invented a technology that is capable, useful and scalable. That was a breakthrough.  However, what came next was not. 

With all of its limitations and potential harm to the society, Microsoft, Google & Amazon jumped right to scalability, instead solving its evident limitation: energy costs. Despite the fact, that planned data-centers will consume most of electricity, and energy systems nowhere near to meet that 'artificial demand', they keep pouring billions into the same LLM architecture and actively supressing alternative technologies. We end up coping with all inefficiencies, and what's most important, applications of the technology. 

The data-center buildout in the most cunning possible way of treating the whole planet as the resource for global domination. The technology, that is openly admittted can kill us all, yet nobody can do anything about it. 

Bottom line:

**Organizations are shaping the technologies following their structures - not what people actually need**

## The challenge is clear

How to create an organization, that is capable to sclae the technology, while keep innovating and drive human progress. 

We desparately need alternative organisational models that are equipped to innovate and improve our livelihood. Structure always fights back - small organisations can bring innovations to the market, but when scaled, fallback to the market capture, rather than value.

We need to move faster and smarter. We to build a better future, not more of the same. Considering global challenges that are threatening our natural world and the whole existence of humanity - we don't have a choice. 

It is becoming  evident that we cannot maintain our status-quo and ignore our inner calling for global change. 

### The disruption of organisations to serve our world better is a need of an hour.

N-org is a structural change in the way we collaborate and create *value*. It answers one fundamental question -  _*How to take advantage pace of innovations and management of small and scale it*_. Keep innovating, keep disrupting itself, keep producing tangible value to the society. To serve humanity, not to condition it.

## Networks

Networks are ubiquitous, they are all around us. In fact, networks are at the ground level of the design of our world. Networks operate on all levels of existence, from chemical networks to microorganisms to ecosystems. Nobody manages the network, however the network always finds the most optimal way of operations. 

We see how networks operate on many different levels and scales, showing great resilience, efficiency and diversity. However, we dismiss this knowledge by being conditioned to power and control. We do not use the superpowers of networks. We overlook the benefits of decentralisation and are afraid of it. We think that control is what makes the product better and the process optimal. That people cannot be trusted and cannot work interdependently. Economic structures of competition, debt and artificial scarcity, fake values and control over public resources exacerbate the problem and impede our progress towards a better future. 

Network is not a new model of operations. We see the power of networks in media corporations, where hundreds of companies are creating and distributing content around the world, sharing and exchanging resources - effectively framing and conditioning us. We see highly resilient networks of blockchain, where millions of people operate and collaborate in the network to create and exchange value. Decentralised organisational models are taking on with an attempt to organise collective work in a more fair and productive manner. 

However, we are still enrooted in the old paradigms of control. Even connected by and empowered by networks, we still create rigid organisations that keep working in the old way. We have not developed the know-how of creating network-compatible and network-native organisations. 

N-org is trying to do exactly that - to create a framework of network-native organisation as a design paradigm and a model to follow. 

## What is N-Org ?

N-Org is a meta-organization on a new level of abstraction. C-corps, LLCs, Non-profits, sole-traders - legal structures remain the same. What's changes is the way we organize work, communicate and distribute power.

## The problem

The fundamental change is how we leverage the source of power. Now the power is concentrated with tight hierarchical control - one own company's shares and can coerce anyone in the organisation to do what one want. It’s a straightforward process that we can comprehend and it certainly works to some extent. However, the hard part begins when the company becomes too big. 

The inefficiencies show in inability to control large numbers of units doing different things. For some time it was managable. The market variability was low  “Any customer can have a car painted any colour that he wants so long as it is black.” It worked, until the market became saturated and customer demanded the rainbow colours. At the same time, the new trend of uniqueness and customization has become widespread. 

Tailoring products and services to customer demands added so many variables, that making things on scale become unmanagable. How old structures handled that ? You might have guessed - limit choices and lobby it. Old structure didn't stop working - they became a limiting factor for the whole world They maintain inefficiencies on all levels at the expense of the environment and future generations. 
 
## Solution

With N-org we revolutionise the way we create value on scale, by distributing control and ownership mechanisms, without undermining the integrity. It's not **decentralised**, it's not another DAO or crypto-utopia, it is **distributed capability network** sharing the same goals. 

We shift the power from control to mutual benefit, from rigid `top-down-control` corporations to a distributed network of small organisations, operating in one meta-environment pursuing the goal of the whole 

On the surface, it might look like a sub-reddit or a discord channel, we have already invented it and we know it works.  What's missing is the model of collaboration and a shared goal - the `Umbrella`, that can turn flame into action.

What will follow, is the organizational structure, that is brewing, but not yet established as a technology.
