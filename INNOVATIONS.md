# INNOVATIONS

Innovations are at the core of how I approach my work and life. 

I build only things, that don't exists yet - that can fundamentally improve our experience and the world around us -  that will drive human progress and kickstart next cycle of innovations.

I choose to create fundamental solutions at the lowest abstaction levels. I challenge the mental models, ask deeper questions and observe the root causes of the current systems' inefficiencies and side-effects.

I create novel architectures, methodologies, standards, organizational models and philosophies. 

Always happy to receive your feedback and open to collaboration.
