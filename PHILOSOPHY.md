# Philosophy

Nothing from the past defines who you are today. 

Personal history is just one, and the least important part - the ego part. Part that is imposed by the society, youyr birthplace and language - none of these you choose - even your own name. What truly matters is your personal journey.

Your Personal Journey is immune to outside circumstance. It's uniquely crafted to align with your intention, your mission and your inner calling -  the soul's true purpose. Life guides you through this quest for meaning, mastery, and self-realization.

Your history might seem like a failure to others, but only you decide to be a winner today. Your mindset, your choices, your attitude — these are what matter. Trying to fit in or judge yourself by others’ standards only dims your uniqueness. Embracing everyone’s uniqueness is the source of true power.

Your history isn’t you. The real questions, that define who you are:

- What do you want ? 
- Why do you do what you do? 
- What drives you ?
- What do you believe in ?
- What best you can do today ? 
- What have you learnt ?
- How do you want to see the world ? 
- What do you give ?

What you've been yesterday or the day before, or what you "wish" to be tomorrow are illusions of control, false sense of security. Only what you do today - your actions and your state of mind — define you.

Background, nationality, experience, location - these are just labels, subjective judgments, a mere self-reflection of the observer. Nothing defines who you are today, only sets artificial boundaries for your mind. A construct, an illusion, a self-fulfilling prophecy. You are the choices you make and the actions you take today.

Personal history is just belief in limitation - an excuse `why not`, a distraction. It doesn't exist except in your mind. It distracts you from what you want and can achieve. Attention is priceless; your past and future depend on where you focus.

Impossible is just a test. Over the years, everything I thought was impossible, I achieved. Impossible is only what your mind believes it is - a state of energy, undefined until you choose to define it.
