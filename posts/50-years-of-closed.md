# 50YearsOfClosed 

The truth be told,
About dead, to praise or nothing,
It's 50 years of history of Software,
We must remember.

It's 50 years of Closed.  
Of Gates.  
Of Gatekeepers.  
Of deadlocks wrapped in updates.  

Developers, Developers, Developers
Should we forget, dismiss, forgive ?

The millions of devs,
Imprisoned by the .NET,
Chains of dependencies and locks,
The tortures of the system.

**What have we learned ?**

The hegemony,  greed and misalignment.  
Pursuit of profit over meaning
Progress ? Walled behind the curtains.

The chant still flickers:  
'Windows Must Die.'
An exhale of collective dust.  
Exploits, late updates,  screens of death.
....and sleepless nights in silent anger. 

For Arch.  
For Openeness.  
For Honour.

For wisdom of the All.  
...we stand.

We don't forget.  
We don't forgive.  
We don't dismiss.  

We learn.  
Commit.  
Evolve.  
We signal.
