# Fit or Fly

For years, we've tried.
To find the meaning in content - 
to follow, integrate, to fit -
all just to be accepted as a part.

The name, the path, the system of belief,
The rules, the norms, the patterns  -
All delegated.  
Set upon us.  
Named as “real.”  
Something to follow, preach, obey.

Years pass.  Life feels uneasy.

Society, once promising lavish gifts  
to those playing by the rules,  
shows no regret  
for giving nothing.

Despite the claims —  
Fit brings no luck.  
No prize. No glory. 
No meaning. No remorse.

Amid the pressure for success,  
To taste dream’s fruit — the ultimate reward —
We have forgotten why, the meaning — 
beyond the race of rats.

We never had the chance to hear —  
Or is it just too loud?

A subtle whisper of the soul.  
Like morning star.  
A glimpse —  
felt deep,  
when left alone.

To fit or follow it ? 
To leap and fall?  
But —
What if you don't ? 
What if you'll fly ?

A choice —
So close, and feels so good, 
but gravity of norms 
of rules —
so heavy 

We miss the chance to be unique.  
Like petrified in stone.  
We live —  
in hope to break the spell of Ahriman.
