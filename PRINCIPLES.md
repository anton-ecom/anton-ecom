# Principles

The principles I live by. Each one is a lens for making decisions - in systems, in code and in life.

---

## Architect the Future

The best way to predict the future is to design it. I look into the future, creating things that don't exist... yet.

## Small is Beautiful

I embrace the power and elegance of small, thoughtful actions. Small things move the world.

## Less is More

I achieve more with less. I celebrate constraints. I embrace limitations as opportunities for creativity and innovation.

## Acceptance

You are valuable. I honor your perspective and personal journey. I accept you as you are.

## Integrity

I uphold the highest standards of honesty and moral principles in all my actions, ensuring trust and accountability in my every endeavor.

## Fractal Progress

I design fractal, recursive systems. I recognize natural patterns that repeat at different scales and leverage them to create self-similar, self-organized structures that amplify impact and progress.

## Emergence Design

I look into the systems. I observe and predict their dynamics, their natural tendencies and potential for interconnectedness to guide the design and drive evolutionary progress.

## Patterns

Patterns are everywhere. I recognize and leverage patterns in systems and nature.

## Change the Game

When everything else fails, I change the game. I seek to redefine the rules and create new possibilities.

## Do One Thing and Do It Well

I focus on excellence in one area, choosing quality over quantity.

## Caterpillar

There is nothing in a caterpillar that tells you it is going to be a butterfly. I celebrate the wonder of nature and the potential for transformation of everything and everyone.

## The Trim Tab

Focusing on high leverage points and addressing root causes, I effect significant changes, even in complex situations.

## Wu Wei (無為)

I embrace the principle of effortless action, aligning myself with the natural flow of events. By acting in harmony with the environment and circumstances, I achieve optimal outcomes with minimal resistance.

## Mastery

I embrace the relentless pursuit of excellence, continuous learning, and the unbounded journey of self-improvement.

## HIME — High Impact Minimum Effort

I choose actions with highest impact and focus my energy on actions that yield the greatest results with minimal effort.

## 99/1 — Miyazaki Principle

Just like a Miyazaki mango tree, stripped from its fruits to yield the best, I focus on achieving the greatest impact by prioritizing efforts on 1% of high-leverage actions and removing everything else.

## Seikukan (正確感)

I embody a profound sense of accuracy in all my actions. My well-defined processes, precision, and meticulous attention to detail ensure excellence and legendary quality in every aspect of my work. I master my actions with an unwavering commitment to uphold myself to the highest standards.

## Tensegrity

I recognize the interconnectedness and balance within complex systems. By embracing tension and structure simultaneously, I achieve resilience, adaptability, and transformative outcomes.

## Mount Fuji

I aspire to embody the strength and vision of Mount Fuji. With a solid foundation that withstands life's challenges, I rise to heights that make even the grandest endeavors seem small. My elevated perspective grants me clarity to see beyond the immediate, understanding the forces that shape events near and far. Like Mount Fuji, I stand unwavering, offering insight and guidance in an ever-changing world.

## Hysteresis

By learning from the system's history, I gain insights to effectively address and solve problems it has created.

## Mutualism

I prioritize collaboration and building relationships in the system. I choose closer partnerships, reciprocity, and shared goals to achieve success.

## Perspectivism

I value and incorporate diverse viewpoints and experiences to enrich my worldview and make well-informed decisions.

## Collective Intelligence

I seek and harness the wisdom and knowledge of groups to make better, more informed decisions.

## Synergy

I create value through collaborative efforts, where the combined effect is greater than the sum of individual contributions.

## Master of Abstractions

I think in terms of layers of abstractions, understanding how different levels of complexity interact and influence each other.
